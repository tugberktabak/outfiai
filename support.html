1. Welcome to Outfi Ai Support

Thank you for choosing Outfi Ai! We're here to help with technical problems, subscriptions and credits, or any feedback you'd like to share.

2. How Outfi Ai Works

Outfi Ai changes the clothes in your photo: upload a photo of yourself, choose an outfit or garment, and the App generates a new image of you wearing it. Images are generated by AI models through fal.ai. Each generation uses credits, which come with a subscription or a credit pack. Your generated images are kept in your history, and you can delete them at any time. See our Privacy Policy for details.

3. Common Issues and Solutions

  • Image Generation Failure: Make sure you have enough credits and a stable internet connection, then try again. Restart the App if the issue persists.
  • Credits or Subscription Not Showing: Use "Restore Purchases" in the App. Contact support if the problem continues.
  • Canceling a Subscription: Open iOS Settings › your name › Subscriptions, select Outfi Ai, and tap Cancel Subscription. Cancel at least 24 hours before the renewal date.
  • Refunds: All purchases are processed by Apple. You can request a refund at reportaproblem.apple.com.
  • Slow Performance: Check your device storage and network speed.
  • Upload Errors: Use a clear, well-lit photo in a common format (JPEG, PNG, or HEIC).

4. Contacting Support

For assistance, please email us at tugberktabak@yahoo.com. Include your user ID (found in Settings), a description of the issue, and any screenshots to help us resolve it faster. We aim to respond within 48 hours.

5. Privacy and Data Requests

To request access to or deletion of your data, email us with your user ID from Settings. You can delete your generated images yourself at any time in the App.

6. Feature Requests and Feedback

Share your suggestions or report bugs by emailing tugberktabak@yahoo.com. Your feedback helps us improve the App.

7. Troubleshooting Tips

  • Make sure you have the latest version of the App from the App Store.
  • Check your internet connection, as a weak signal may affect generation.
  • If you run out of credits, you can get more with a subscription or a credit pack.
