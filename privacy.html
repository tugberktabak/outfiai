Privacy Policy

Last Updated: November 16, 2026

This Privacy Policy explains what data the Outfi Ai app ("App", "we") collects, why we collect it, and who we share it with.

1. Information We Collect

The App does not ask for your name, email address, phone number, or precise location. No account or registration is required. When you first open the App, a random anonymous user ID is created so that your credits, purchases, and generated images can be linked to your device.

We collect the following data:

  • Photos you upload and the images generated from them, to perform the clothes change you request.
  • Identifiers: the anonymous user ID and device identifiers (such as the identifier for vendor). The advertising identifier (IDFA) is collected only if you allow tracking in the App Tracking Transparency prompt.
  • Usage data: how you interact with the App (for example screens viewed, buttons tapped, features used) and advertising attribution data (which ad campaign, if any, led to your install).
  • Purchase history: subscriptions and credit purchases. We never receive your payment card or billing details.
  • Diagnostics: crash reports and performance data.

2. Photos and Face Data

Photos you upload may contain face data (facial features visible in the photo). This data is processed only to generate the image you request.

Photos and face data are not used for identity recognition, biometric profiling, authentication, advertising, tracking, or analytics, and are never shared with advertising or analytics partners. No biometric identifiers, faceprints, or facial landmarks are extracted or stored.

Image generation is performed by AI models provided through fal.ai, with requests handled by our n8n workflow and backend infrastructure provided by Supabase.

3. Data Storage and Retention

Generated images are stored so that you can access your results in your history. We keep them only as long as needed to provide this feature.

Data is stored on Supabase infrastructure hosted on Amazon Web Services (AWS) Asia Pacific – Sydney (ap-southeast-2).

You can delete generated images at any time in the App, which removes them from your history and our database. You can also ask us by email to delete all data linked to your user ID. fal.ai processes images only to complete your request, under its own data retention policy.

Analytics and attribution data are kept by the respective providers according to their own retention policies.

4. Third-Party Services

We share data with the following providers only for the purposes described:

  • Supabase – backend, database, and storage (AWS Asia Pacific – Sydney, ap-southeast-2)
  • fal.ai – AI image generation
  • n8n – workflow automation that processes generation requests
  • RevenueCat – subscription and purchase management; receives the anonymous user ID, purchase history, ad attribution data, and device identifiers
  • Google Firebase (Google Analytics) – app analytics; receives the anonymous user ID, device identifiers, and usage data
  • Meta (Facebook SDK) – advertising measurement; receives app events such as installs, app opens, and purchases, and the advertising identifier if you allow tracking
  • Apple Search Ads – attribution of installs that come from Apple Search Ads campaigns (via Apple's AdServices framework, which does not use the advertising identifier)

We do not sell your personal data.

5. Analytics, Advertising, and Tracking

We use the services above to understand how the App is used and to measure how well our advertising campaigns work.

If you allow tracking in the App Tracking Transparency prompt, your advertising identifier, purchase events, and app interaction events are shared with Meta to measure and optimize our ads. Under Apple's definition, this counts as tracking. If you do not allow tracking, the advertising identifier is not collected or shared.

You can change this choice at any time in iOS Settings › Privacy & Security › Tracking.

6. Payments and Subscriptions

All payments are processed by Apple through In-App Purchases and managed via RevenueCat. We do not collect or store payment card details or billing information.

7. Data Security

We use industry-standard security measures, including encrypted connections (HTTPS), when processing and storing data.

8. Your Rights and Choices

  • Delete any generated image, or your whole history, in the App at any time.
  • Turn tracking on or off in iOS Settings at any time.
  • Request access to, or deletion of, the data linked to your user ID by emailing us with the user ID shown in the App's Settings.

Depending on where you live (for example under the GDPR or CCPA), you may have additional rights. Contact us to exercise them.

9. Children's Privacy

Outfi Ai is not intended for children under the age of 13. We do not knowingly collect data from children.

10. Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy to reflect legal, technical, or operational changes. The "Last Updated" date above shows when it was last changed. Continued use of the App after updates indicates acceptance of the revised policy.
